Echo Warrior · Recreation Tech

Effective: June 1, 2025
TRANSMISSION BEGINS

01 // OVERVIEW

Echo Warrior ("the Game", "we", "our") is developed by Recreation Tech. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play the Game on your Android device, how it is used, and what controls you have.

By installing or playing Echo Warrior, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the Game.

02 // INFORMATION WE COLLECT

The Game does not require account registration and does not directly collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The following data may be collected automatically through our third-party service providers:

  • Gameplay events — game start/over, wave completions, skills used, scores (Firebase Analytics)
  • Device information — device model, OS version, language, screen size (Firebase Analytics)
  • App diagnostics — crash reports and performance data (Firebase)
  • Advertising identifiers — Android Advertising ID (AAID) used by AdMob for ad personalization
  • IP address — used by Google services to determine approximate country/region
  • Consent preferences — your ad consent choices as recorded by Google UMP SDK

All game scores and preferences (best score, joystick settings) are stored locally on your device only using Android SharedPreferences and are never transmitted to our servers.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED NO DIRECT PII COLLECTED SCORES STORED LOCALLY

03 // THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Echo Warrior integrates the following Google / Firebase services. Each operates under Google's Privacy Policy.

FIREBASE ANALYTICS

Used to understand how players interact with the Game (events like game start, wave complete, skill usage). Data is aggregated and anonymized. Google may use this data consistent with their policies.

→ Firebase Privacy Information

GOOGLE ADMOB

We display rewarded video ads via AdMob. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID and related signals to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings under Privacy → Ads.

→ Google Ads Privacy Policy

GOOGLE UMP (CONSENT SDK)

We use the Google User Messaging Platform to collect your consent for personalized advertising where required by law (e.g., GDPR in the EU/EEA, CCPA in California). Your consent choice is stored on-device and sent to Google.

→ Google Privacy Policy

04 // HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Improving gameplay balance and user experience through aggregated analytics
  • Diagnosing crashes and technical issues
  • Serving rewarded video advertisements to support free access to the Game
  • Complying with legal obligations regarding advertising consent

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party outside of the services listed above.

05 // ADVERTISING & YOUR CHOICES

Echo Warrior shows rewarded ads (you watch a video to earn an in-game revive). These ads are served by Google AdMob.

You have the following controls:

  • Opt out of personalized ads: Go to Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Reset Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID.
  • Consent form: On first launch in GDPR/CCPA regions, a consent form is shown. You may withdraw or change consent by reinstalling the app.

Non-personalized ads may still be shown based on contextual signals (e.g., app category) rather than your profile.

06 // CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Echo Warrior is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the Game, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

07 // DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

We do not operate our own servers storing your personal data. Data collected by Firebase and AdMob is retained according to Google's own data retention policies.

Local data (scores, settings) remains on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear the app data.

Google employs industry-standard security measures to protect data processed through their services.

08 // YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data collected about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw advertising consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

For requests related to data held by Google (Firebase, AdMob), please use Google's data controls at myaccount.google.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
